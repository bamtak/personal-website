P-01

Noovera Trade

The AI Retail OS for Africa: point of sale, stock and credit records for informal merchants, on one phone, offline.

Market traders and corner shops run sales and stock from memory or paper. That leaks revenue through stockouts and credit, and leaves no records a lender can trust. Noovera turns an Android phone into the shop's daily operating system.

  • Offline-first Flutter app. LitePOS checkout, stock and day-end reports are held locally and reconciled with the cloud when the network returns.
  • Voice-first assistant. Speech-to-text and intent parsing turn a spoken sale into a transaction. I evaluated on-device models against a cloud-primary policy and recorded the decision in an ADR.
  • Web command centre and storefronts. Owner analytics, multi-shop comparison and credit exposure in React, with pickup-only online storefronts, Paystack subscriptions and a token wallet.

P-02

FarmPro

An AI-native farm operating system for Africa: maps, satellite monitoring, operations, finance and agronomy agents in one workspace.

Satellite data, weather, soil science, inventory, money and team work rarely sit in one place, so decisions come late and profitability stays opaque. FarmPro is the single record from land definition through harvest and sale.

  • Field intelligence. Draw or walk a boundary, attach crops, and let daily satellite scans surface vegetation anomalies early.
  • Agent layer. Agronomics, Proactive Field, Vision (disease and pest identification from photos), Planner and Finance agents, plus a 30-minute heartbeat that checks weather, price moves, NDVI and pest risk.
  • Operations and money. Multi-farm workspaces for crops, poultry and trade, fertigation, livestock, inventory, team payments, waybills, invoices and cooperative tools.

P-03

OmniEnergy

OmniEnable takes a Nigerian solar installer from site visit to consolidated equipment delivery, on a phone.

Installers lose hours per proposal to Excel and Word, quotes go stale when prices move, and equipment comes from several distributors with no consolidated order or warranty trail.

  • Five-step workflow. Drop a pin, design the system, send a proposal, close the deal, receive equipment in one order.
  • Site capture and design. Satellite map with roof polygons and offline-capable cached tiles, codified sizing rules with visible assumptions, voltage-drop cable sizing and templated single-line diagrams.
  • Customer proposal pages. A shareable link with no login, bill of materials, warranty and payment terms, and OTP approval. The brief targets under 15 minutes per proposal against 2+ hours today.

P-04

WattHouse

Trade pricing, orders and investor reporting for a technical distributor of electrical, solar and energy-storage equipment in Lagos.

A working prototype on a low-code front end and Supabase triggers had access-control gaps: staff roles could see admin pages the API then refused. The business needed a typed, auditable system it could trust with orders and money.

  • Re-platformed backend. I reconstructed the database semantics from the prototype and rebuilt them as a NestJS API with typed contracts, a global JWT guard and a documented role matrix.
  • Two price lists. Retail pricing, and trade pricing unlocked by installer verification, with a cost-floor guard and director-only overrides.
  • Order to handover, and investors. Orders, invoices, part-payment receipts and serial-number handover, plus import cycles with term sheets and profit distributions in an investor portal.

P-05

BuildLink

The trust and cash operating system for construction: from BOQ to payment, backed by proof from site.

Money on construction projects moves on disputed claims and unverifiable site evidence, and owners abroad cannot see progress. BuildLink ties the commercial record to site evidence, so payment follows certified, proven work.

  • Commercial spine. BOQ import, valuations and IPC certificates with retention, then certify, approve, record release and record paid.
  • Package-scoped access. Clients and QS see every work package, contractors see only theirs, and contractor margins, stock and payroll stay private.
  • Site evidence. QA/QC hold points with e-signature, GPS-tagged tamper-evident photos, an offline field PWA and no-login links for artisans.
  • Regulatory-aware. It records attestation and never holds funds. A data note maps GPS and presence data to the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

P-06

Arrivox

A marketplace for cross-border vehicle trade between North America and Lagos: list before the car ships, find verified shippers, settle through one ledger.

Buying a car overseas and shipping it home is fragmented. Listings, deposits, freight quotes and paperwork sit across chats and intermediaries, and trust is the bottleneck, especially for diaspora buyers.

  • AutoList. Sellers list vehicles before they arrive, buyers register interest, the seller ranks offers and the chosen buyer commits with a 5% deposit.
  • ShipMyCar. Freight quotes from platform-approved shippers, with an internal rate-management tool.
  • Trust and compliance. KYC with identity-verification providers, FINTRAC-aligned AML and KYC guidelines, a wallet ledger, Stripe payments, disputes and ratings.

About

Electrical engineering first, then machine intelligence

I trained as an electrical engineer, moved into software, then studied machine intelligence on a Google and Facebook scholarship. That mix shows in the work: products for clinics, farms, shops and construction sites, where a system that fails costs real money or real care.

How I build

Six habits that make the results dependable.

Contracts

Web, mobile and API that cannot drift apart

Typed contracts (ts-rest and Zod) published as versioned packages, so a breaking change fails the build instead of a customer’s phone.

AI

AI that earns its place in production

Agents with feedback loops, vision for crop disease and invoices, voice commands, and on-prem models when data cannot leave the site.

Connectivity

Works when the network does not

Offline-first mobile that reconciles later, cached map tiles, and no-login links for people who have no accounts.

Trust

Money and identity, done carefully

Paystack and Stripe, KYC providers and wallet ledgers, shaped by FINTRAC and the Nigeria Data Protection Act. Attestation, not custody, where a licence would apply.

Access

Private data stays private

Role matrices and package-scoped visibility, so every user sees exactly their slice and nothing more.

Delivery

Handover-ready from day one

Every repository ships with verify scripts, CI, briefs, decision records and seeded demo data, so anyone can run it and demo it.
